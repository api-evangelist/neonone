— KEY TAKEAWAYS Start with six priority segments: major donors, loyal mid-level donors, recurring donors, first-time donors, lapsed donors, and engaged non-donors like volunteers and event attendees. They’re practical, scalable, and they’ll carry you through December and beyond. You don’t need all six, but you should use at least four.
